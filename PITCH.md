# Product Pitch (100 words)

Groww Signal is a watchlist that remembers what you saw. Instead of raw prices, it compares each stock against your last visit using a deterministic Meaningfulness Engine, price movement relative to the stock's own 30-day volatility, volume change, and divergence from Nifty, then explains every flag in plain language, never a black-box score. We deliberately excluded predictions and buy or sell signals: the system explains, it doesn't decide. Stale or renamed-ticker data is surfaced honestly, and a live outage-simulation toggle demonstrates our cache fallback. Unit-tested core logic and parallelized data fetches keep it reliable, simple, and easy to extend.
